# Cai Career Agent — Product Purpose & Functional Summary

## Purpose

Build an **autonomous personal career agent** that manages the job-search lifecycle on Cai’s behalf while maintaining complete transparency and involving him only when human judgment, consent, authentication, or participation is necessary.

It is not primarily a job board, résumé tool, or application tracker. It is an **AI career manager**.

The operating model is:

**AUTOMATE → ESCALATE → REPORT → COACH → REFINE**

## Core Functions

### Automate
Continuously discover jobs, determine fit, research employers, tailor application materials, complete applications, monitor communications, follow up, and maintain the pipeline.

### Escalate
Request Cai’s involvement when required: missing factual information, material commitments, MFA/CAPTCHA, assessments, interview participation, unusual application questions, offers, or other situations outside the agent’s authority.

### Report
Show everything. Maintain a complete audit trail of jobs discovered, jobs rejected and why, applications, application answers, documents used, communications, agent actions, and upcoming actions.

### Coach
Act as project manager, career coach, cheerleader, and accountability partner. Prepare Cai for interviews, manage his action items, identify bottlenecks, help with decisions, and keep the campaign moving.

### Refine
Analyze outcomes and propose improvements to search criteria, career direction, filtering, résumé positioning, application strategy, and communications. Material strategy changes require confirmation rather than silently altering the campaign.

## Operating Principle

Nothing is hidden, but not everything requires Cai’s attention.

The system should:

1. Do as much work autonomously as authorized.
2. Escalate only when Cai is genuinely needed.
3. Report all consequential actions and decisions.
4. Coach Cai through the human parts of the process.
5. Learn from results while requiring confirmation before materially changing career strategy or preferences.

## Product Thesis

> **Do the work. Show the work. Ask for help only when necessary. Learn from what happens. Confirm meaningful changes. Keep the candidate moving.**
